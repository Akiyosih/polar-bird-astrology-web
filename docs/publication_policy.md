# Publication Policy

This repository is public. Keep it suitable for readers, contributors, and
community members.

## Public Field Notes

Field Notes may discuss symbolic timing, community mood, project rhythm, and
interpretive themes.

They must not present astrology as:

- investment advice;
- official Core communication;
- a guarantee of any product launch, market result, or technical outcome.

## Private Data

Do not commit client profiles, birth data, raw chart data, private reports,
payment records, sessions, logs, secrets, or internal planning notes.

## Public Copy

Public copy should be concise, readable, and specific about what each surface
does. Boundaries should usually be handled through accurate claim scope,
omission of unsupported factual claims, and placement on the right explanatory
page, not by repeating defensive disclaimers on brand surfaces.

For Japanese homepage and UI copy, follow `docs/homepage_copy_policy_ja.md`.
Avoid publishing internal workflow details that are not useful to the public
reader.
