# Publication Policy

This repository is public. Keep it suitable for readers, contributors, and
community members.

## Public Field Notes

Field Notes may discuss symbolic timing, community mood, project rhythm, and
interpretive themes.

They must not present astrology as:

- investment advice;
- official Core communication;
- a guarantee of any product launch, market result, or technical outcome.

## Private Data

Do not commit client profiles, birth data, raw chart data, private reports,
payment records, sessions, logs, secrets, or internal planning notes.

## Public Copy

Public copy should be concise, readable, and clear about boundaries. Avoid
publishing internal workflow details that are not useful to the public reader.
